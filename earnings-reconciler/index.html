"How much did I actually make this month?"

If you sell on more than one platform, the honest answer usually takes an evening and a spreadsheet.

Every platform counts differently

Gumroad, Redbubble, Etsy and PayPal each report sales, fees, refunds and payouts their own way, in their own columns, sometimes in different currencies.

Adding up dashboards counts money twice

Redbubble pays into PayPal, and some Gumroad sales settle there too. Add PayPal to your platform totals and the same money appears twice. Many accounting-sync tools focus on Amazon, Shopify and Etsy, and a tool that looks at one platform at a time can't see this.

Your accountant wants one tidy file

Not a stack of exports and a note saying "the PayPal one is in dollars". Month-end and year-end shouldn't take a weekend.

How it works

Download your exports

Export your sales, statement or activity CSV from each platform you use. We'll show you where to find each one (some, like Redbubble and Etsy, arrive by email).

Drop them in

Drag the files into the page. It recognises each platform's format, lines up sales, fees and refunds, and matches payouts to sales across platforms. Anything it can't place goes to a short review list instead of being guessed.

Get your report

See your profit by platform, product and month, plus a check of which payouts arrived. Download an accountant CSV, or bank-statement files for Xero and QuickBooks Online.

It stays in your browser

Your exports contain your customers' names and emails, and your income. We don't want them, and the app is built so we never get them.

  • Files are read and processed on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to us, and the app tells your browser to block uploads to anywhere else.
  • Buyer names, emails and addresses are dropped on import and never stored. The app keeps only what the report needs: amounts, dates, products, platforms, order IDs and buyer country (as a country code).
  • Your report data is saved in this browser on this device. Download a backup file any time and restore it later or on another computer.
  • No accounts, no cloud database, no analytics, no cookies. The app goes online only to check your licence key and download exchange rates. Neither request includes your sales data.

FAQ

Is this tax or accounting advice?

No. Reporting tool only. Not tax, accounting, legal or financial advice. Check the figures against your platform statements and ask a qualified accountant before you file anything.

The app calculates no tax. Where a platform reports tax it collected itself (for example Etsy or Gumroad), that amount is shown in its own column, labelled "Tax as reported by [platform]. Not calculated or checked by us.", and never counted as your income.

What does "nothing counted twice" mean?

Redbubble pays your earnings into PayPal, and Gumroad sales paid through a connected PayPal account settle there too. If you just add up each platform, those amounts appear once as a sale and again as PayPal income. The app links these payouts to the sales they came from, so they count once. Anything it can't match goes to a review list where you decide, and it's never hidden inside your profit.

Do you see my sales data?

No. The app runs entirely in your browser. Your CSV files are processed on your own device and never sent to us. We don't run a database of anyone's sales.

Where is my data kept?

In your browser's own storage on your device. It isn't synced to other devices. If you clear this site's data, it's gone, so the app lets you download a backup file and restore it later, including on another computer.

Does the app go online at all?

The app goes online only to check your licence key and download exchange rates. Neither request includes your sales data. Your sales files and reports are never sent anywhere.

How are currencies converted?

You choose your reporting currency. Each transaction is converted at the European Central Bank's euro reference rate for its date, fetched through the free Frankfurter service. Where a platform already converted the money (such as PayPal currency conversions), its actual amounts are kept. The accountant CSV shows the rate, rate date and source on every line, and you can enter a rate by hand if one is missing.

Why not just use a spreadsheet?

You can, and many sellers do. The hard part is that every platform's export is different, changes now and then, and some (like Redbubble's) only go back 12 months. The app keeps a parser for each platform up to date, matches payouts across platforms and handles currencies, so you don't rebuild your formulas every month. Files it doesn't recognise can be mapped by hand.

Does it connect to my platform accounts?

No. It works from the CSV files you download yourself, so you never give us your logins or API keys.

Does it work with Xero and QuickBooks?

It creates bank-statement CSV files laid out for importing into Xero and QuickBooks Online, one file per platform account. It doesn't log in to your accounting software or post anything for you. You decide what goes in.
