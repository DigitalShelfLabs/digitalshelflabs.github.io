For one-file sellers with no audience: promote your product in online groups without getting removed or banned.

Your file is live. Nobody knows it exists. The people who need it are already asking for help in groups you can join. The first time you post your link there, it gets ignored, removed, or you get a warning.

This kit is for showing up in those groups the way they allow: give one useful piece of your file away whole, say that you made it, and mention it only where the rules let you.

What is inside:

  • The rules card: 10 questions per group, about ten minutes, ending in one verdict. Green: post. Yellow: ask the moderators first. Red: do not mention your file.
  • Mine your file: six prompts and a three-question test to find the one page you can give away that makes people want the rest.
  • Six post scripts with brackets to fill: one-step tutorial, mistake list, worked example, checklist excerpt, feedback post, promo-thread entry. Each works with no link at all, and comes with four endings matched to the group's verdict.
  • Four reply scripts for threads where someone already asked your question, plus when to say nothing about your file.
  • Three messages to moderators: before you post, which thread to use, and after a removal.
  • Disclosure lines, a list of hype phrases with what to write instead, and a 12-item do-not-post list.
  • A 14-day plan (about 30 minutes a day, three groups), a posting log, and one fictional worked example from rules card to post.

Who it is for: you sell one PDF, template, or small file, with no audience and no ad budget.

Who should skip it: you want a way around a group's rules, bulk posting, or DM outreach. None of that is here. Some groups will say no, and this kit helps you find that out before you post.
