Privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

This policy covers this website, digitalshelflabs.github.io, run by DigitalShelf Labs. Questions: digitalshelflabs@proton.me.

What this site collects

Nothing that we can see. This is a static website made of plain HTML pages, images and a CSS file. It has no sign-up forms, no comments, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking pixels, and it loads no third-party scripts or fonts.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit any website your browser sends technical data, such as your IP address, the page requested and your browser type, so the server can deliver the page. GitHub may log this data to keep its service secure. We don't receive or have access to these logs. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.

Buying on Gumroad

When you click "Buy on Gumroad" you leave this site. Checkout, payment and file delivery happen on Gumroad, under Gumroad's privacy policy. As the seller, we receive the order details Gumroad shares with sellers (such as your name and email address and what you bought) and use them only to deliver your purchase, answer support requests and handle refunds. We don't sell this data or send marketing emails unless you've asked for them.

Links in the RSS feed and on social platforms

We publish an RSS feed at /feed.xml so platforms such as Pinterest can show our product images. If you reach us from Pinterest or another platform, that platform's own privacy policy applies to what you do there.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply, and we delete correspondence we no longer need.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK), you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Email digitalshelflabs@proton.me and we'll reply within 30 days.

Children

This site isn't aimed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
